# Glossary

Terms from the text that a player may not know. Each entry is one plain sentence, written to
sit under a verse in small text. This file is the data for the verse footnotes now and the
Journal's Lore tab later; keep entries short and add to it as new khans bring new words.

| Term | Note |
|------|------|
| Kayanid | Of the Kayanian kings, the dynasty Rostam serves; Kay Kavus is its king during the seven labours. |
| Mazandaran | The land of the divs, where Kay Kavus is held captive and where the seven labours lead. |
| gorz | Rostam's ox-headed mace, his signature weapon in the Shahnameh. |
| Rakhsh | Rostam's horse, chosen by him as a colt and his companion through every labour; the name means radiant. |
| Rostam | The hero of the Shahnameh's heroic age, son of Zal, champion of the Kayanian kings. |
| Shahnameh | Ferdowsi's Book of Kings, completed around 1010, the epic of Iran's myths and history in verse. |
| Haft Khan | The seven labours: the trials Rostam passes on the road to Mazandaran to free Kay Kavus. |
| div | A demon of the Persian tradition; the divs of Mazandaran are the enemies of the seven labours. |
| babr-e bayan | Rostam's tiger-skin coat, his armour in the text. Not yet in the game. |
| Zal | Rostam's father, raised by the Simorgh. Not yet in the game. |
| Simorgh | The great bird that raised Zal and aids his line. Not yet in the game. |

## Rules

- One sentence per entry, plain, no spoilers beyond the khan the player has reached.
- A term is matched by its English spelling as it appears in the verses file, so keep the
  spelling consistent between the two files.
- The same file feeds the Journal later; entries marked "Not yet in the game" stay hidden
  until the term appears on screen.
